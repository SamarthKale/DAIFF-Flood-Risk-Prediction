# Problem Statement 3

**AI-Based Deforestation Monitoring and Flood Risk Prediction Using Sentinel-2 and Climate Data**

Team: Rugved Hinge, Samruddhi Bhaskar Doke, Samarth Kale, Abhinaya Gowda (HPC Research Internship).
Contact details are deliberately not reproduced in this public repository.

## Problem

Deforestation and extreme weather conditions are increasing the risk of floods and environmental
damage in many regions. Traditional methods of monitoring forests and predicting floods are often
slow, manual, and unable to process large amounts of satellite and climate data efficiently.

## Objective

Develop an AI-based system that uses Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and climate data to:

- detect changes in forest cover over time,
- identify areas affected by deforestation,
- analyse rainfall and environmental conditions,
- predict flood-prone regions using machine-learning techniques.

The system should provide visual maps and early-warning insights that help environmental agencies
and disaster-management authorities take preventive action.

## Expected outcomes

- Deforestation detection maps
- Flood risk prediction model
- Interactive visualisation dashboard
- Early-warning alerts for high-risk areas

## Datasets named in the problem statement

- Sentinel-2 satellite imagery
- NOAA climate data
- Elevation/terrain data from USGS
- Global Forest Watch forest-loss data
